found_something
  yes                     the report states something new about the funded gene
  no                      it does not

if_yes_what
  role                    what the gene does in cells or animals
  disease link            the gene is tied to a disease
  mechanism               how the gene works or is controlled
  location                where the gene or protein is found
  binding partners        what the gene's protein binds
  new ligand              a new compound that acts on the gene's protein
  structure               the protein's 3D structure

if_no_why
  tools only              made tools, but no finding
  no finding stated       the report gives no finding
  other gene              the finding is on a different gene
  experiments failed      the main experiments did not work
  negative result         tested it and saw no effect
  stopped early           work halted before the grant ended
  covid19                 COVID-19 stopped the work
  delayed due to covid19  COVID-19 delayed the work
  plans only              only future plans reported
  no text                 no outcome report
  unclear                 cannot tell which results came from this grant
